## Samsung running sweat estimate

Implemented from Samsung's [sweat-loss procedure](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/tracking-sweat-loss.html), [profile contract](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/api-reference/com/samsung/android/service/health/tracking/data/TrackerUserProfile.Builder.html), [permissions](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/permission-request.html) and [result/status contract](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/api-reference/com/samsung/android/service/health/tracking/data/ValueKey.SweatLossSet.html). No equivalent collector existed in the older project. Orbit reuses its current workout service, sensor lease/cleanup, saved profile delivery, SQLite journal, exact-hash outbox and authenticated Data Layer.

Running requests Health Services cadence when supported. Before starting the exercise, Orbit snapshots the existing profile, queries the actual Samsung capability and attaches the SDK tracker. The Health Services API start is followed by Samsung START; confirmed pauses/resumes propagate to Samsung. Original monotonic cadence times are converted using the run's native clock anchor, with bounded batches, duplicate suppression and rejection of changed samples, future times and clock shifts. Samsung's SDK enum is `STEP_PER_MINUTE`; Health Services uses `STEPS_PER_MINUTE`. No cadence is guessed from distance or calorie totals.

The ordinary workout ends and commits at its actual Health Services time before waiting up to 15 seconds for the Samsung result. The foreground notification remains during that wait. State/profile/status/raw finite value/SDK timestamp and native receipt times are retained for accepted final results. SDK status zero alone qualifies a finite nonnegative prediction; failures never become a displayed zero, intake record or hydration recommendation. Samsung's positive status bits are explained (short run, low distance/energy/estimate, heart/cadence gaps, initialization failure, low cadence), while unknown codes remain visible. Early results, malformed results, clock shifts, missing profile/permission, unsupported service and interrupted collection stay unavailable rather than being promoted into measurements.

`SweatJournal` stores a bounded sequence of at most three immutable revisions per run in the existing database. Its installation/workout/boot/start identity also validates if the attachment arrives before its workout. Capture and outbox commit atomically; altered duplicates, changed profile and reversed state are rejected. `/orbit/v1/workout-sweat` has its own capability and retry queue, so a phone awaiting an update still receives ordinary workouts/readings. An orphaned tracking/pending record becomes explicitly interrupted under the free sensor lease. SDK cleanup cannot release ownership before the final durable write; failed saves retain the result for the service's retry action. App-private data remains excluded from Android backup.

The phone's existing workout details and the Watch's finished-workout/history details show the attachment; there is no new long list or profile form. The Watch locale follows configuration changes. Callback registration and service teardown share a narrow lock so cancellation during the longer optional startup does not leave an attached callback.
