## Edge-local lens on the indicator — 12 September 2026 02:21 (Claude)

The user's reaction to the first pass was that it still looked the same, with a reference image of a frosted bar carrying a bright refractive blob. That was a fair reading: frost alone diffuses, and on Orbit's near-black panels a diffusion of nothing looks like nothing.

**The engine can refract.** `verification/perf-tools/refraction-probe.cjs` renders a checkerboard under three capsules — blur only, blur plus an SVG displacement filter, and opaque — and measures the pixels. `backdrop-filter: url(#filter)` is accepted *and produces displaced pixels*: the blurred-only capsule flattens the pattern to a mean 147.2 with deviation 12.6, while the refracted one keeps visible structure at deviation 15.59 with the pattern bent toward the edges. **What shipped.** One shared `#glass-lens` filter is inlined in the page: the displacement map's normal is neutral through the middle and deviates only in a tenth of the shape at each edge, at scale 0.07 of the bounding box, so the background bends where the glass curves and stays put in the centre. There is no interior magnification and no channel splitting. `.has-glass-lens` is set only when the engine accepts a filter reference in a backdrop filter; without it the indicator keeps the plain material layer, and the no-backdrop, reduced-transparency and forced-colours fallbacks all clear the lens explicitly.
